 2. GROWTH CONDITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Culture Medium           : ATCC Medium 1511: Vitamin B6 blood agar ATCC Medium 260 with 10.0 mg/L of pyridoxal-HCl
  * Incubation Temperature   : 37°C
  * Atmosphere               : Aerobic
  * Handling Procedure       : Open vial according to enclosed instructions.Using a single tube of #1511 broth (5 to 6 mL), withdraw approximately 0.5 to 1.0 mL with a Pasteur or 1.0 mL pipette.  Rehydrate the entire pellet.Aseptically transfer this aliquot back into the broth tube.  Mix well.Use several drops of the suspension to inoculate a #1511 agar slant and/or plate.Incubate the tubes and plate at 37°C for 24 hours.
  * Handling Notes           : This strain has been sequenced as a reference genome for the NIH Human Microbiome Project.Additional information on this culture is available on the ATCC®web site at www.atcc.org.
  * Storage Conditions       : 2°C to 8°C
  * Product Format           : Freeze-dried
